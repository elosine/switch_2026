These fit ONE harmonic series: 211 · 316 · 472 · 632 = 4 · 6 · 9 · 12 × **52.6 Hz**, which is **G♯1 (+23 cents)**. With 367 (7) and 422 (8):
**the C3 petal IS a G♯1 chord — G♯3 · D♯4 · F♯4(−31c) · G♯4 · A♯4 · D♯5**, its loudest note the 9th partial A♯4. The design's 35 Hz with
spread 1.33 happens to land on that series (the partials 46.5 Hz apart from 175 Hz); the bed decided which of them sing.

**A4** (designed: fund 200, first 2, spread 0.5, a quarter-tone copy): peaks 395 (0 dB) · 417 · 493 · 592 · 694 · 788 · 881 · 917 —
a plain harmonic series on **G2 (99 Hz)**, partials 4 … 9, with its quarter-tone shadow.

So the two petals he has used are a **G♯ series and a G series: roots a semitone apart** — the neighbors of Recombination's semi-cluster.

## 3 · The method, from Recombination (piece 5, LG-18 … LG-28)

- A SET of neighboring fundamentals, a semi-cluster of roots: **F♯1 · G♯1 · A1 · B♭1 · B1 · C2** — the roots themselves are the leitmotif.
- A PATH through them, not a scale: B♭1 · A1 · C2 · G♯1 · B1 · F♯1 — it starts in the middle, alternates outward, ends at the bottom.
- Each chord = PARTIALS of its root, the colored ones for the just players (5 · 7 · 10 · 11 · 14), the bass on the fundamental; the tempered
  instruments double at the nominal pitch and **beat at the partial's deviation** (−14 · −31 · −49 cents) with no player doing anything.
- Transitions between reference chords: spectral · balance · bloom · converge (LG-28).

## 6 · Proposal A — home · away · home · under (recommended)

| # | time | fundamental | the chord (partials → pitch, cents) | why here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 275 … 325 | **G♯1** | 4 G♯3 · 6 D♯4 · 9 A♯4 (+4) · 12 D♯5 | the rise: the players enter inside the petal's own series, nothing rubs |
| 2 | 325 … 355 | **C2** | 5 E4 (−14) · 7 A♯4 (−31) · 10 E5 (−14) | the first hills: a step away that keeps A♯ and adds the E the petal hints at |
| 3 | 355 … 371 | **F♯1** | 7 E4 (−31) · 9 G♯4 (+4) · 11 C5 (−49) · 14 E5 (−31) | the valley: the root drops a whole tone under home; the E now −31, the C5 a quarter-tone |
| 4 | 371 … 414 | **B♭1** | 5 D4 (−14) · 7 G♯4 (−31) · 8 A♯4 · 11 E5 (−49) | the two big hills: the petal's loudest note (A♯4) becomes the chord's octave — the strongest away-chord |
| 5 | 414 … 430 | **A1** | 5 C♯4 (−14) · 7 G4 (−31) · 8 A4 · 11 D♯5 (−49) | the friction chord before the return: A against A♯, G against G♯ |
| 6 | 430 … 457 | **G♯1** | 4 · 6 · 7 F♯4 (−31) · 8 · 9 · 12, + 11 D5 (−49) | the loudest hills: home with the full series and its quarter-tone |
| 7 | 457 … 477 | **G1** | 4 G3 · 6 D4 · 9 A4 (+4) · 13 D♯5 (+41) | the ring dies: the series slips a semitone beneath it — the other petal's root, the bottom of the path |

The path **G♯1 · C2 · F♯1 · B♭1 · A1 · G♯1 · G1** runs through the semi-cluster F♯ · G · G♯ · A · B♭ · C (Recombination's set, G1 in
place of B1): it starts at home, alternates outward (up a major third, down a whole tone, up a whole tone, up a semitone), returns, and
ends one step under, as Recombination's path ended at its bottom.

**Who plays just:** the winds and the strings — the samplers take the bend per note (the recipes' `bendRangeSt`), so a partial's cents
are sent, not approximated. The piano and the vibraphone double tempered and beat at the deviation (−14 · −31 · −49), piece 5's effect.
**The bass of each chord:** the cello on the root's octave (G♯2 · C3 · F♯2 · B♭2 · A2 · G♯2 · G2) if he wants Recombination's
"bass on the fundamental"; otherwise the cello stays on its hills.

## 7 · Proposal B — the drift (the simpler alternative)

The same seven containers; the root rises a semitone each time and falls back: **G♯1 · A1 · B♭1 · B1 · C2 · G♯1 · G1**. Easier to
hear as a line; less tied to what the petal actually sounds (B1 shares almost nothing with it), and the friction chord (A1) comes first
rather than before the return.

## 8 · The one decision

A, B, or a different path through the same six roots. The timing (the hills) and the partial sets are then written into the save by a
tool (the chords as struck or shaped notes on the players' lanes, the cents as bend), his to hear.
