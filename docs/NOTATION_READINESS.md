## What has to be done before a whole-piece page (the port's remainder)

1. **The four electronics lanes, in the notation.** The page format's limit (parts up to 9), the registry's layer numbers (it still says
   the composer's extra layers sit at 8 … 11; they are at 12 … 15 now), and the registry's parts. *The lesson of the earlier ports: a lane
   NUMBER written into a module is invisible to a search by name (H6-13) — each place is read, not grepped.*
2. **A drawing for the material brick** — the electronics on their own lanes are this piece's new thing; no earlier piece has it. A DEVICE
   SHEET first (how a stretch of a sound file is shown to the players and the technologists), then its rule rows.
3. **Six techniques registered:** alto sax `double_tonguing` · `slap_exploding` · `slap_pitched`; percussion `brake_drums_rubber_mallets` ·
   `gran_cassa_medium_felt` · `gran_cassa_rute_on_rim`. Each needs its written word or sign. Until then the extractor stops at the first one.
4. **The sample lengths of the short articulations** (`staccato` · `glock_shorts_hard` · the strings' staccato): the notation draws a short
   note at its measured length; these were never measured here, so it falls back to the drawn length with a warning on every note. Not a
   blocker; the look of 3,000 short notes depends on it.
